Questions sellers ask

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. HeroShot removes the background with Apple's on-device Vision framework. Your photos and the finished images stay on your iPhone until you choose to save or share them.

Which iPhones does HeroShot work on?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 17 or later.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited background removal and every marketplace preset, exported at up to 1080 pixels on the long side with a small HeroShot mark in the corner. Pro removes the mark and exports at each marketplace's full size.

What kinds of products work best?

Anything with a clear outline: shoes, bottles, jars, candles, jewelry, electronics, toys, packaged goods and folded clothing. Good light and a little space around the product give the cleanest edges.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then HeroShot, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open the paywall in HeroShot and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID. More help is on the support page.
