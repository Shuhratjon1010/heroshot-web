Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Applies to the HeroShot iPhone app, version 1.0 and later, and to heroshot.app.

The short version
  • Background removal runs on your iPhone. Your photos and finished images are never uploaded to us.
  • You can use HeroShot without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we keep an account ID and the email Apple gives us, only to run your account and subscription.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card.
  • No ads, no ad identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

1. Your photos

When you take or choose a photo, HeroShot uses Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks on your device to separate the product from its background and render the export. The original photo, the cutout and the exported image stay in the app's storage on your iPhone. We do not receive them, store them, look at them or use them to train any model.

HeroShot asks for access to your camera and photo library only when you use those features, and only to read the photos you pick or save the images you export. You can change this at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security.

When you share or save an export, it goes where you send it (for example Photos, Files or a marketplace app). What happens there is governed by that app's policy.

2. What we collect

If you never sign in, HeroShot does not send us any personal data. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we store:

DataWhy
A user identifier from Apple, unique to HeroShotTo recognise your account when you sign in again or on another device.
Email address, if you choose to share it (often an Apple private relay address)To contact you about your account or a support request. We don't send marketing email.
Subscription status (plan, start and renewal dates, Apple transaction ID)To unlock Pro on your account.

We never receive your real name unless you share it with Apple's sign-in sheet, and we never receive your password, phone number, contacts or location.

3. Payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple shares with us only what is needed to confirm your subscription, such as the product, dates and a transaction ID. We never see your payment card or billing address.

4. Stored only on your device

HeroShot keeps a few settings on your iPhone, such as whether you finished onboarding, your last marketplace and background choice, and a cache of recent images. These never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

5. Analytics, ads and tracking

HeroShot 1.0 contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not access the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with us under Apple's own privacy policy.

If a future version adds analytics, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before it ships, and offer a way to opt out.

6. Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent personal data. Account data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, which processes it on our behalf. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect the safety of users.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data while your account exists. To delete it, open HeroShot, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. That permanently removes your account data from our servers. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in the Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions. You can also email privacy@heroshot.app and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, including to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority. Email privacy@heroshot.app and we'll respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Our legal basis for processing account data is providing the service you asked for (contract).

9. Children

HeroShot is a tool for sellers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. This website

heroshot.app is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad scripts. Our host, Cloudflare, processes basic request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

11. Security

Account data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our provider. Because your photos never leave your device, they're protected by your iPhone's own security.

12. Changes

If we change how HeroShot handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For material changes we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@heroshot.app. Everything else: Support.
