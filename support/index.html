I paid for Pro but it isn't unlocked

  1. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought Pro with (Settings, then your name).
  2. In HeroShot, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  3. If Pro still doesn't unlock, force-quit HeroShot, open it again and repeat step 2. Then email us with the date of purchase; please don't include card details.

How do I cancel Pro?

Apple manages all subscriptions. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then HeroShot, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes refunds for App Store purchases, so we can't issue them directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find HeroShot and choose "Request a refund".

The cutout missed part of my product

  • Shoot in even light and avoid strong shadows falling on the product.
  • Leave some space around the product so its whole outline is in frame.
  • Use a background that contrasts with the product: a white mug on a white table is hard to separate.
  • Glass, very thin parts (like cables or chains) and fuzzy edges are the hardest. Try another angle or a darker background.

If a photo still won't cut out cleanly, email it to us if you're happy to share it. It helps us improve.

Where are my exports saved?

Tap Export to open the share sheet. Choose Save Image to put it in your Photos library, or send it to Files, AirDrop, Mail or a seller app. HeroShot names each file after its marketplace preset so it's easy to find.

Why is there a HeroShot mark on my image?

Free exports include a small mark in the corner and are limited to 1080 pixels on the long side. HeroShot Pro removes the mark and exports at each marketplace's full size.

Do you see my photos?

No. Background removal runs entirely on your iPhone and your photos are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I need to run HeroShot?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

How do I delete my account?

If you signed in with Apple, open HeroShot, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account data from our servers. It doesn't cancel an Apple subscription; see how to cancel. If you never signed in, there's no account to delete: just delete the app.

Sign in with Apple isn't working

You don't need to sign in to use HeroShot. If you want to and it fails, check that you're connected to the internet and signed in to iCloud in Settings, then try again. If it still fails, email us with the time it happened.
